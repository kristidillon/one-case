Premium mandibular advancement devices

Two premium nylon appliances.

Two premium options so you can personalize treatment for every patient. Both feature 0.5 mm incremental adjustments and lightweight, metal-free construction.

Best fit for most patients

Preferred Nylon MAD

The Preferred Nylon MAD is a traction-style advancement system designed to encourage mouth closure during sleep. It supports nasal breathing while minimizing mandibular drop. The lightweight two-piece nylon construction offers exceptional comfort with a completely metal-free design and minimal bulk.

Recommended for patients with

  • Mouth breathing tendencies
  • TMJ sensitivity
  • A need for a durable nylon alternative with greater titration range and adjustability

Freedom Nylon MAD

The dorsal fin push-style mechanism on the Freedom Nylon MAD allows for greater mandibular opening freedom during sleep. This design maintains effective advancement and precise titration capabilities. The lightweight, metal-free nylon design improves patient comfort and allows for easier speaking and drinking compared to traditional appliances.

Recommended for patients with

  • A desire for greater jaw mobility
  • The need for posterior comfort
  • A preference for simplified adjustment capabilities

What your hygienist runs

The 3-Minute Airway Checklist.

A quick-reference for transitioning from clinical observation to a life-changing conversation.

1 · The observation

Clinical Red Flags

During your routine exam, look for these key indicators of potential airway issues:

  • Tooth wearModerate to severe occlusal attrition (grinding).
  • Soft tissueScalloped tongue or Mallampati Class III–IV.
  • StructureHigh/narrow palate or lower anterior crowding.
  • ProfileRetrognathic jaw (recessed mandible).

2 · The conversation

The Bridge

Connect your clinical findings to the patient’s daily life.

The opening

“I’m noticing a couple of things in your mouth that we sometimes see in patients who aren’t breathing optimally during sleep. Can I ask you a few quick questions about that?”

The quick screen

  • Do you feel rested when you wake up?
  • Has anyone ever told you that you snore?
  • Do you wake up with headaches/dry mouth?
  • Have you ever had a formal sleep study?

3 · The Clarity Sleep Ring

The Solution

Offer a low-friction path to clarity. Patients want to feel better; you are providing the tool to get them there.

The pitch

“We can screen this easily. You’ll take home a small ring to wear while you sleep for one night. A sleep physician reviews the data virtually to see if a custom device—a comfortable, quiet alternative to a CPAP—could help you finally wake up rested.”

The action
